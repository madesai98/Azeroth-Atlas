    Something went wrong

    Report a problem

    Say what went wrong. Your words go into one zip file together with what may help find the cause: this computer, this copy of Azeroth Atlas and its notes of what it did, the game and its addons. You are asked where to save it. Nothing is sent anywhere: the file is yours to pass on.

    Is it about the game? Type /reload in the game before you save the report. The addon keeps notes of what it did, and the game writes them to the disk only on a /reload or when you log out.
    What goes into the file, and what never does
    • This computer: the version of Windows, the processor, memory, graphics card and its driver, drives and how full they are, the antivirus program, and the names of the programs running (an overlay or a capture program can be in the way).
    • Azeroth Atlas: its version and a digest of each of its own files, its settings, its notes of what it did (the game found or lost, each route handed to the game and whether the game read it, errors), what the map was showing when you made the report, and the route mailboxes as they stood.
    • The game: its build and settings, the addons installed and which are switched on, what this addon has saved (with its own notes) and what the addons it works with have saved (Questie, Guidelime and guide packs, TomTom, Shortest Path Forever, DockWatch and the like), the game's own logs about itself and its addons, the beginning of its last three crash reports.
    • Never: account names, passwords, e-mail addresses, your Windows user name and the computer's name (struck from every text and path); the game's chat and combat logs; your macros; crash dumps; anything of the game itself. Character and realm names are in it: the game keeps what addons save under them.
