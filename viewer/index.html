    Flying — mouse to look, WASD to move, wheel = speed, Esc to stop

    Controls

    DragMove the map
    Right-drag / Alt-dragRotate and tilt around the point under the cursor; while moving with the keys it steers instead
    WheelZoom toward the cursor
    Double-clickZoom in on a point
    ClickDrop a marker on what is under the cursor (Esc clears it); where there are several levels at that spot, you are asked which
    Shift-clickDrop a quick marker: no question, the level nearest your own height
    W A S D / arrowsMove  ·  Shift = faster
    Q / ETurn left / right
    R / F  (Space / C)Up / down
    GFree-look flying (mouse look, wheel changes speed)
    N  /  TFace north  /  top-down view
    XCutaway: open up whatever hides your character (a building or a hill in the way, the ceiling of the room they are in, the ground over a city under it, the tree tops around them)
    BFold the sidebar away, or bring it back
    FollowingRight-drag and Q / E look around your character, the wheel zooms on them. Dragging the map or moving with the keys stops following; the round button at the bottom right starts it again
